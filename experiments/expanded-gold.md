# Expanded retrieval evaluation: scope and provenance

`eval-expanded.mjs` fixes 16 queries and checks at least one expected normalized phrase among up to four evidence items, or an empty result for the two absent-data probes; every returned item must have the requested year and budget type. It also records evidence hashes, asset reads and timing. Queries cover 2007 XLS facility names and spacing, 2024/2025 settlement, 2025/2026 initial budgets, different program names, 2099 missing year and nonsense. It does not establish every returned result's relevance, distinguish all same-page businesses, verify numeric column alignment, or measure corpus-wide precision/recall. Empty 2099 search means no evidence in this snapshot, not proof of absence. Several labels were derived from snapshot source text; they are not an independently blinded or statistically representative evaluation set.

Raw benchmark runs: `expanded-confirmed-before.jsonl`, `expanded-confirmed-after.jsonl`. Five fresh Node processes each, fixed query sequence, then immediate repeat per query. First calls after the first scenario may reuse assets loaded by earlier scenarios. Before and after had 15/16 and 16/16 expected-phrase/filter checks respectively in each of five runs. The single changed case is the explicit `및` disaster query. Evidence hashes were identical for all other 15 cases in all five paired runs; the disaster result changed intentionally. The test is a scoped presence check, not an assertion that every hit is correct.

The two shard-heavy warm cases, in-process median of five: 청년월세 22.11→0.53 ms, semiconductor 24.80→0.49 ms; four asset reads→zero. The cache is bounded to 64 entries and 1,000,000 bytes of serialized values (keys and object overhead are extra), expires after five minutes, and keys include exact query, location, passage bounds and ranking scores. It is per isolate, ephemeral, not persistent; memory pressure, concurrent load, production cold starts and tail latency have not been tested. Samples are too small to generalize. Actual first-call medians in those cases were 154.16→155.66 ms and 152.75→149.54 ms, respectively.

The spreadsheet unit mapping was changed from one embedded `if` to a reviewable data manifest and generic exact-match lookup. There is still only one independently reviewed row; no automatic same-sheet or cross-sheet inference is enabled. Lookup requires document ID, document hash, block number and text hash, and conflicting duplicate records fail closed. Synthetic tests cover distinct sheets/units, mismatched block/hash and duplicate records. Generalization requires separate original-sheet header/row review, versioning, corpus reindex strategy, and negative tests before adding records.
